Static vs. continuous batching

Same 8 requests, same 4 GPU slots. Static batching holds every slot until the longest request in the batch finishes before admitting new work. Continuous batching replaces a finished slot immediately — no batch boundary to wait for.

Chunked vs. unchunked prefill

Three requests (A, B, C) are decoding steadily when a fourth request with a long prompt arrives. Without chunking, its entire prefill runs as one uninterrupted block — stalling everyone else's decode. Chunking breaks it up and interleaves it, trading a slightly later finish for D against eliminating the stall for A, B, and C. (Schematic, not cycle-accurate.)
